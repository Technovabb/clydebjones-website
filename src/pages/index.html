Since 1948

Three generations. One promise.

On 13 October 1948, Clyde B. Jones and his wife Alma took their first call. The next day, they directed their first funeral.

Mr. Jones was 25. He set a standard of care, honesty and dignity that his children and grandchildren still keep today. In 1994 the family chose burgundy as the company's colour, to move away from the traditional black.
