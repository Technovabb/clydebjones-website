Burial at sea

Burial at sea

A rare and meaningful farewell for those who loved the sea.

How it works

Burial at sea is rarely requested, but we can arrange it. It needs:

  • The right documents and permissions.
  • Following the environmental rules.
  • Special equipment to weight the casket.
  • A vessel, hired for the burial.

Please talk to us early, so there is time to get everything in place.

A service before

Many families hold a funeral or a memorial service on land first, so everyone can say goodbye. We can help you plan both.
