Repatriation

Bringing your loved one home

Whether a loved one died overseas, or wished to rest in another country, we take care of the arrangements with care and patience.

If a death happens overseas

Even when a death happens away from home, call us first. We work with the funeral home where your loved one died, take care of the transport, and bring them home to Barbados.

Calling from the USA or Canada?

(716) 262-2407

Our USA & Canada line reaches our team in Barbados.

Taking a loved one to another country

If your loved one is to be buried in another country, tell us as early as you can, so we can begin the arrangements with the proper authorities.

Meeting another country's rules takes time. Sometimes this means a number of days, or even weeks. We will keep you informed at every step.

Sending ashes overseas

If your loved one has been cremated, we can help with the papers needed to send the ashes to another country.

Insurance that covers travel

Some bereavement insurance plans cover the care and transport of a loved one who dies away from home. This can be a big expense, so it is worth checking. We can explain how these plans work and point you to the right companies.

Family who cannot travel

Family abroad can still be part of the day. They can watch the service live, leave a message in the condolence book, and send flowers, all from the tribute page.
