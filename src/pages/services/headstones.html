Headstones and gravework

A headstone marks the resting place of your loved one for generations to come. We help you choose it, order it and care for it.

What we do

  • Marble and granite headstones.
  • Grave building and painting.
  • Grave refurbishing.
  • Polishing headstones and repainting the letters.
  • Ceremonial plaques.
  • Wooden grave markers.

See our headstone collection (PDF, 12 pages)

Choosing a headstone

If your family owns a plot, a headstone may be on your list of things to do. Call us and we will help you choose and order it.

  • Markers lie flat on the ground. Some cemeteries ask for these.
  • Monuments stand upright.

Both come in many sizes, shapes and colours.

If you do not own the plot

Where a burial is without ownership of the plot, a temporary grave marker may be placed on the grave for five years, after getting permission from the proper authorities. We can help with this.
