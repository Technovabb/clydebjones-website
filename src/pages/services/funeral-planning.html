Funeral planning

A farewell that will always stay with you

We follow your loved one's last wishes (if they left any), the laws of Barbados, and your own wishes and budget.

How we plan together

You will sit down with one of our funeral directors or co-ordinators. We listen first. Then we explain your choices, one at a time, and write everything down so nothing is missed.

Our complete package lets you personalise every part of the funeral, meets all your needs, and keeps you in control of what you spend. We explain every cost before you decide.

Burial or cremation

One of the first choices is burial or cremation. It shapes the other choices, such as the type of service and the resting place.

  • Ground burial: the casket is buried in a family plot or a new plot.
  • Above-ground burial: some cemeteries have crypts in a mausoleum.
  • Cremation: see our cremation page.
  • Burial at sea: rarely requested, but we can arrange it. See burial at sea.

Good to know: when you buy a cemetery plot, you buy the right to be buried there (the "right of interment"), not the land itself.

The services you can choose

  • Viewing or visitation: usually the night before, or just before the funeral. The casket can be open or closed.
  • Funeral service: at your church, or another place that meant something to them.
  • Memorial or celebration of life: family and friends gather to remember, usually without the casket.
  • Graveside service: final words, prayers and memories at the grave.

You can have one service, or several.

Making it personal

Every funeral should be as unique as the life it honours. Here are some questions to think about:

  • Where should the service be held?
  • Who should lead it?
  • Should it follow the traditions of a faith or culture?
  • Will there be a eulogy, and who will give it?
  • What music, readings or poems would they have wanted?
  • Are there photos or keepsakes to put on display?
  • Should the decorations show a hobby they loved, like fishing, gardening or music?
  • Will there be refreshments or a reception afterwards?

Flowers

We can arrange flowers for the service. Friends and family can also send a floral tribute from the tributes page.
