Livestream & photos

No one has to miss the goodbye

Family and friends anywhere in the world can watch the service live, and watch it again later.

Watching the livestream

  1. Open your loved one's tribute page. Find it on our tributes page, or tap the link the family shared on WhatsApp or Facebook.
  2. At the time of the service, the livestream plays at the top of the page.
  3. Missed it? The recording stays on the same page, so you can watch it again.

Tip for families: share the tribute page link on WhatsApp before the service, so everyone knows where to watch.

Photos and video

Ask us about photography and video of the service, so your family has something to keep.

Leaving a message from afar

While you watch, you can leave a message in the condolence book on the tribute page. The family will see it once our staff have checked it.
