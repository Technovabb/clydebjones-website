Our story since 1948

Three generations. One promise.

Clyde B. Jones Funeral Home Co. Ltd. has served Barbadian families from the same home in Top Rock, Christ Church, since 13 October 1948. Our motto has never changed: “Where honour dwells and service excels.”

How it began

A husband and wife, and a $60 funeral

The idea came from Clyde's brother-in-law, the late Kenneth “Puncheon” Parris, a former Inspector of the Royal Barbados Police Force. In October 1948, Clyde B. Jones and his wife, Alma, made it real. It was not an easy time to start a business.

Their first funeral was for a lady from Oistins Hill, Christ Church. It cost $60. With no modern refrigeration in those days, everything, from the arrangements to the burial, was done within 24 hours.

Like other newcomers, known then as “briefcase” or “suitcase” undertakers, they relied on the established firms. The late Harold A. Tudor of Tudor's Funeral Home, The Ivy, St. Michael, loyally helped them, renting out his motor hearse, or the popular horse-drawn hearse driven by the famous Bajan character “Bottleneck”.

In the last six weeks of 1948, the new firm conducted 16 funerals: 15 at the churchyard of Christ Church Parish Church, and one at the neighbouring Christ Church Cemetery.

Our founder

Clyde B. Jones, BCH, JP 1923 – 2008

A humble man of few words, but a giant in all he did: husband, father, grandfather, businessman, organist and “jack of all trades”. He entered the funeral business at just 25, with determination, a strong faith in God, and the support of his wife, Alma.

He led by example. He was precise about detail and record-keeping, and made sure every family was looked after on time, with honesty and dignity. Those standards are still the foundation of our home.

He received the Barbados Centennial Honour in 2000, and was a Justice of the Peace.

Small in size. Great in care.

Trusted by Barbados

Honoured to serve

We have been trusted with a State Funeral for the late Sir Harold St. John, and official funerals for the late Malcolm Marshall, Kamau Brathwaite and Sir Everton Weekes. We have served military and paramilitary funerals, and countless families from all walks of life, here in Barbados and overseas.

We also give back. Over the years we have supported churches, schools, sports and charities, and we run a summer internship programme to help young people, because they are our future.

Our logo

A broken heart, and a lighted candle

Our logo is the letters CBJ, for our founder, Clyde B. Jones. Its simple design reflects the simplicity and humility of the man behind our company.

Look closely, and the C and B form a broken heart, while the J is a lighted candle. Grief leaves us broken-hearted. In fulfilling your wishes, we hope to bring light and hope at the hardest hour.

The candle's three beams of everlasting light are our promise that Clyde B. Jones Funeral Home will always remain a place “where honour dwells and service excels”.
